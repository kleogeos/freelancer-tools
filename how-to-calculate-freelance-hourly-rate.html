How to Calculate Your Freelance Hourly Rate

Most new freelancers take an old salary, divide by 2,080 hours and quote that. It underprices you by 40–60%, because a freelancer does not bill 40 hours a week.

The formula

Rate = ((take-home goal ÷ (1 − tax rate)) + expenses) × (1 + buffer) ÷ billable hours

Example

  • Take-home goal: 60,000; tax rate 25% → 80,000 pre-tax
  • Expenses: 4,000 → 84,000
  • 10% buffer → 92,400 revenue needed
  • 46 working weeks × 40 h × 60% billable = 1,104 billable hours
  • Rate: about 83.70 per hour

Why billable share matters

Sales, admin, invoicing and revisions are unpaid. 50–65% billable is typical for the first years. Track a month of your time to find your real number.
