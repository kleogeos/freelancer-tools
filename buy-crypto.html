Why crypto, and not a card?

Most card/PayPal-based platforms (Gumroad included) currently can't pay creators based in Russia — so a card purchase there would take your money and strand it on the seller's side, undeliverable. Crypto sidesteps that: it settles directly, with no payment processor in the middle to block the payout.
