Why this matters

All three networks can carry USDT, but the cost of actually sending it varies a lot — mostly driven by how busy the network is right now. Ethereum (ERC20) fees spike when the network is congested; TRC20 and BEP20 stay cheap almost always. If your client is picking a network, cheaper usually means TRC20 or BEP20 unless their wallet only supports ERC20.

These are network fees only (what the blockchain charges to process the transfer) — they're paid by whoever sends the transaction, same as a bank wire fee, and are unrelated to the invoice amount itself.
