Free NDA Generator

Fill in, then “Print / Save as PDF”. Nothing is uploaded — everything runs in your browser. For a scoped project after this, try the Proposal generator.

This is a general-purpose template, not legal advice. It covers the basics of a mutual NDA for a straightforward freelance discovery call. For high-value, cross-border, or especially sensitive engagements, have a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction review or draft the agreement instead.
